🕹️ HOW TO PLAY (3D MODE):

  • WASD / Arrow Keys: Walk & navigate around the cyberpunk bunker.
  • Shift: Sprint for high-speed locomotion.
  • Spacebar: Jump over conduits or obstacles.
  • Mouse Look / Drag: Rotate 360° camera orbit. Scroll to zoom.
  • Key E: Walk close to any glowing workstation and press E to hack into the terminal.
  • Key V: Switch between Third-Person and First-Person camera views!

🎯 CTF CHALLENGE GOALS:

There are 6 stations inside the bunker:

  1. Web Exploitation: SQL injection authentication bypass.
  2. Cryptography: Multi-layer ROT13 and Base64 cipher decoder.
  3. Linux PrivEsc: Fully simulated interactive bash shell.
  4. Digital Forensics: Inspect raw network packet hex streams.
  5. Reverse Engineering: Decompiled mathematical serial keygen puzzle.
  6. Omega Supercomputer: Central boss vault. Unlocks once you acquire 500+ PTS!
